Registration is now open for the Digital Archaeology Teaching Labs for the 2025-2026 academic year, organised by LAD: the Digital Archaeology Lab at Sapienza, in collaboration with the [Sapienza Archaeological Mission in Albania](../../ricerca/missione-archeologica-sapienza-a-cuka-e-ajtoit-albania/); they aim to provide the theoretical and practical foundations needed to set up and independently develop your own **GIS** projects and to engage with the world of digital cartography and the graphic processing of archaeological monument surveys.

The labs will be held **in person** at Sapienza Università di Roma, **Aula IV**, on the first floor of the Faculty of Letters and Philosophy building (CU003), on **26, 27, 28, 29, 30 January and 2, 3 February 2026**, from **14:00 to 18:00**.

Coordinator: **Julian Bogdani** ([julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it](mailto:julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it))

## CFU

Where applicable, attendance of **all** modules of the lab entitles participants to a certificate of attendance that can be used to obtain 2 CFU, to be recorded with the relevant contact person for each degree programme. Additional hours of independent work can be arranged to obtain further CFU.


## Other important information

The classroom is **not** equipped with computers, so participants are **required to bring their own**.

It is **strongly recommended** to have a mouse available for the lab activities.

[QGIS](https://qgis.org/) will be used for the lab activities — free and open-source software **supported by the most widely used operating systems** (Windows, MacOS, and various Linux distributions). It is recommended to download and install the latest LTR (Long Term Release) from the [official page](https://qgis.org/download/), which is currently **3.40 Bratislava**.


Finally, participants must have access to Sapienza WiFi or Eduroam, both available in the classroom.
