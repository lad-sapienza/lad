> **Please note**: Detailed information on the dates of the next excavation and research campaign at Çuka e Ajtoit (Southern Albania) for **2025** is not yet available. Depending on administrative matters, the campaign may indicatively take place in **June** or **September 2025**.  
> It is nevertheless possible to express your interest by filling in the form available at the following address: [https://forms.gle/jFn8eYrDEm4G9r1f8](https://forms.gle/jFn8eYrDEm4G9r1f8)

As part of the activities of **LAD: the Digital Archaeology Lab** at Sapienza and of the **Italian-Albanian Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit** (Southern Albania), training internships will be organised over the coming autumn-winter months of 2024-2025, concerning the digital archaeological documentation of the latest field research campaigns, reserved for Sapienza students. Some of the planned activities include:
- producing 3D digital models using the photogrammetric technique (Structure from Motion, software: Agisoft Metashape), 	
- vectorisation and preparation of graphic documentation (plans and elevations) of archaeological data in a GIS environment (software: QGIS)
- structuring online bibliographic databases (software: Zotero)

The activities will take place in the LAD's premises, on the third floor of the Faculty of Letters and Philosophy building (CU003), and, in part, independently; they grant training credits (AAF).

> **To express your interest, please send an email to Prof. Julian Bogdani at: [julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it](mailto:julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it)**.
