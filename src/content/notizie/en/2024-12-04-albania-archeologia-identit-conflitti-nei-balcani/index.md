On Saturday 7 December 2024, from 10 AM to 2 PM, the third meeting "Archaeology, Identity and Conflicts in the Balkans", dedicated to Albania, will be held at [INASA](https://www.inasaroma.org/) (Istituto Nazionale di Archeologia e Storia dell'Arte, Piazza di San Marco, 49), as part of the series of meetings on Archaeological Missions in the Mediterranean: historical research, identity and conflicts past and present.

The day is curated by L. M. Caliò and F. R. Fiano - Italia Nostra.

**Programme**

**_The contribution of Italian archaeology in Albania_**  
**Luigi Maria Caliò**, University of Catania.
> Full Professor of Classical Archaeology, he is the author of the volume "Romanitas. L'antichità romana e il fascismo tra Italia e Albania" and curated the exhibition "La presenza italiana in Albania tra il 1924 e il 1943. La ricerca archeologica, la conservazione, le scelte progettuali".

_**Archaeology and Identity in the Balkans**_  
**Maja Gori**, CNR - Institute of Heritage Science

>Senior researcher and member of the Mission archéologique franco-albanaise du bassin de Korçë, she is director of the journal Ex-Novo. Journal of Archaeology, for which she edited "Balkan Archaeology as a Laboratory" and "The Impact of the fall of communism on european heritage".

**_Critical Heritage: the heritage of the communist regime_**  
**Francesco Iacono**, University of Bologna Alma Mater Studiorum

>Associate Professor; for the Cultural Heritage of Dictatorship in Albania project, he is the author of "Exploring the Public Perception of Communist Heritage in Post-communist Albania" and "Revolution and counter-revolution; or why it is difficult to have a heritage of communism and what can we do"

**_Excavating at Çuka e Ajtoit: the project, the institutions and the communities_**  
**Julian Bogdani**, Sapienza University of Rome

> Associate Professor of Archaeological Research Methodology, he has directed Sapienza's mission at Çuka e Ajtoit since 2021 in collaboration with the Albanian Institute of Archaeology of Tirana; the project involves Italian and Albanian students.


A discussion with the audience will follow.
