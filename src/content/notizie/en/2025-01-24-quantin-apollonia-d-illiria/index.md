The [Sapienza Archaeological Mission in Albania](../../ricerca/missione-archeologica-sapienza-a-cuka-e-ajtoit-albania/), as part of the Classical Archaeology Seminars of the [Sapienza Università di Roma Doctoral School in Archaeology](https://phd.uniroma1.it/web/ARCHEOLOGIA_nD3482_IT.aspx), is pleased to organise a meeting with **François Quantin** entitled
"Research at Apollonia in Illyria by the Franco-Albanian Archaeological Mission".

The seminar will be held on **25 February 2025**, from **2:00 to 4:00 PM** in **Aula IV, Faculty of Letters and Philosophy** (CU003, first floor).

[François Quantin](https://www.ephe.psl.eu/francois-quantin) is professor of _Religions de la Grèce ancienne: espaces, cultes, institutions_ at the École Pratique des Hautes Études - PSL and co-director of the Franco-Albanian mission at Apollonia (Institute of Archaeology of Tirana and the École Française d'Athènes - EFA).

The seminar will open with a welcome address by the doctoral school coordinator **Maria Teresa D'Alessio** and will be introduced by **Julian Bogdani**.  
A discussion with the audience, moderated by **Julian Bogdani** and **Enrico Giorgi**, will follow.

**Students and colleagues are invited to attend**.
