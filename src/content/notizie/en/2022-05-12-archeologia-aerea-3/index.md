From 19 to 21 May 2022, at the Museo Castromediano, the University of Salento's Department of Cultural Heritage and Laboratory of Ancient Topography and Photogrammetry, in collaboration with the National Research Council's Institute of Heritage Science and the Laboratory of Ancient Topography and Archaeological Cartography, are organising the Third International Conference on Aerial Archaeology dedicated to the theme: **"The Invisible Cities". Remote and Proximal Sensing in Archaeology: non-invasive methodologies for the study of the ancient city**.

LAD is taking part with a paper by Julian Bogdani entitled _Documenting the invisible. The Çuka e Ajtoit project: survey and documentation of a hilltop site_.
