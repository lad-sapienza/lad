On **20 and 21 November 2023**, members of the [Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit](../../ricerca/missione-archeologica-sapienza-a-cuka-e-ajtoit-albania/), Julian **Bogdani** and Nadia **Aleotti**, will take part in the international conference "Between History and Archaeology: Corcyra and the Epeiros. New Data and Research Perspectives" at the University of Calabria. The event programme follows.

**The conference proceedings can be followed _online_ via the Microsoft Teams platform. Please send a request to [julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it](mailto:julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it) to connect.**
