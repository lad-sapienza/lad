On 7 April 2022, LAD director Julian Bogdani will be at the [Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II](https://www.unina.it), at the invitation of the [Scuola di Specializzazione in Archeologia](http://www.ssba.unina.it/) and as part of the series of Seminars on Topography and Cartography curated by Rodolfo Brancato, to speak on “**Digital Archaeology 2022: theory, methodologies, data, tools. Disordered notes to make sense of it all**”.

The meeting will take place on 7 April 2022, at 10:30 AM, in Aula 709, Via Nuova Marina 33, Naples.
To attend in person, please write to [rodolfo.brancato@unina.it](mailto:rodolfo.brancato@unina.it). The meeting will be broadcast online via Microsoft Teams at the following link: [https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meetup-join/19%3aCQc8jobbduSdReBR7PY4jxlD1fmjUHJ3NBarIzZ_n0k1%40thread.tacv2/1648910725946?context=%7b%22Tid%22%3a%222fcfe26a-bb62-46b0-b1e3-28f9da0c45fd%22%2c%22Oid%22%3a%2268cfbebf-d8da-4586-be1a-b8c24be0be10%22%7d](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meetup-join/19%3aCQc8jobbduSdReBR7PY4jxlD1fmjUHJ3NBarIzZ_n0k1%40thread.tacv2/1648910725946?context=%7b%22Tid%22%3a%222fcfe26a-bb62-46b0-b1e3-28f9da0c45fd%22%2c%22Oid%22%3a%2268cfbebf-d8da-4586-be1a-b8c24be0be10%22%7d)
