Between **11 and 13 April 2024**, the director of the [Sapienza Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit](../ricerca/missione-archeologica-sapienza-a-cuka-e-ajtoit-albania/), Julian Bogdani, will take part in the international workshop "Current Research on Illyria and Epirus. 4th century BC – 1st century AD" organised by the University of Trier (Germany), with a paper entitled _Chaonia in Epirus from the Archaic Period to the Hellenistic Age_.

The workshop is organised as part of the ADRIA project – Die adriatisch-ionische Küstenzone der Balkanhalbinsel in hellenistischer Zeit (P.I. Prof. Frank Daubner).
