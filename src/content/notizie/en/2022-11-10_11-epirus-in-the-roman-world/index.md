On **10 and 11 November 2022** the directors of the [Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit](../../ricerca/missione-archeologica-sapienza-a-cuka-e-ajtoit-albania/), Julian **Bogdani** and Albana **Meta**, will take part in the conference "Epirus in the Roman World and the New Research on the Acropolis of Butrint by the Albanian Institute of Archaeology and the University of Bologna", organised by the University of Bologna and the Albanian Institute of Archaeology, with the paper “**Kestrine: new data for the long-term archaeology of a border region**”.

The conference will be held in the Aula Gambi, Department of History, Cultures, Civilizations, University of Bologna, Piazza San Giovanni in Monte, 2, and will be broadcast *online* on the Teams platform, at: [https://tinyurl.com/y27md4ff](https://tinyurl.com/y27md4ff).

For more information, please contact [federica.carbotti@studio.unibo.it](mailto:federica.carbotti@studio.unibo.it) or [anna.gamberini3@unibo.it](mailto:anna.gamberini3@unibo.it).
