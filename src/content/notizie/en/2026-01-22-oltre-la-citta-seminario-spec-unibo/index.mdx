On 4 February 2026, at 2:00 PM, the co-director of the Sapienza Archaeological Mission in Albania, Prof. Julian Bogdani, will give a seminar at the [School of Specialisation in Archaeological Heritage of the University of Bologna](https://spec.unibo.it/beni-archeologici/it) entitled: "Beyond the city: the fortified settlement of Çuka e Ajtoit, in Cestrine".

The seminar is part of the course **The Great Negotiation. Archaeology of the Romanization of the Balkans: The Case of Epirus**, curated by [Prof. Giuseppe Lepore](https://www.unibo.it/sitoweb/giuseppe.lepore4), and will be held online, via [Microsoft Teams](https://teams.microsoft.com/meet/32266776397397?p=aOWnDmWTqgXDgf5xy9).

For information on accessing the virtual classroom: [giuseppe.lepore4@unibo.it](mailto:giuseppe.lepore4@unibo.it).
