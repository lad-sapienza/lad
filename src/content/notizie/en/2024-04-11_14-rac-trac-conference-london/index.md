Between **11 and 14 April 2024**, members of LAD: Laboratorio di Archeologia Digitale at Sapienza, Julian **Bogdani** and Eleonora **Iacopini**, will take part in the international conference "RAC / TRAC (Roman Archaeology Conference / Theoretical Roman Archaeology Conference) 2024" in London, in session 2, dedicated to "Urban Structures, Inscriptions and Interaction in Imperial Rome: new approaches".
The conference will present updates on research carried out in collaboration with the [ERC Advanced project In-Rome](../ricerca/inrome/), P.I. Prof. Barbara Borg, Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa, with a paper entitled "Digital infrastructures for the understanding of Roman archaeological landscapes".

The full session programme is available on the [UCL website](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/archaeology/news-events/conferences/ractrac-conference-2024-timetable/ractrac-session-2-urban-structures), the event's organiser.
