On 2 December 2025, at 3:00 PM in Aula Gualandi, Lorenzo Cardarelli will give a lecture at the University of Bologna on the topic: "AI in the service of Archaeology. The PyPottery example".


The lecture is part of the activities of the "Prehistoric materials lab" curated by Florencia Debandi, and will take place within the **Prehistory and Protohistory** course of the master's degree in **Archaeology and Cultures of the Ancient World**.
