On **13-15 November 2024**, the director of the Italian-Albanian Archaeological Mission (Sapienza - Albanian Institute of Archaeology) Julian Bogdani will take part in the international conference "Butrinto Cento (1924-2024). Achievements and prospects", organised at the DISCI Department (Piazza San Giovanni in Monte 2, Bologna) by the University of Bologna and the Albanian Institute of Archaeology, with a paper entitled _Research at Çuka e Ajtoit_.

Also speaking at the same event for the Mission is Nadia Aleotti (Sapienza University of Rome), with a paper entitled: _What about Pre-Hellenistic pottery? Looking for the 5th and 4th cent. BCE material culture in Northern Epirus_.
