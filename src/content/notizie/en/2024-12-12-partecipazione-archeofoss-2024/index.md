The 2024 edition of ArcheoFOSS 2024, the international conference on Open software, hardware, processes, data and formats in archaeological research, will be held on 19 and 20 December 2024. The eighteenth edition will take place in Chieti, at the University of Chieti-Pescara.

LAD is taking part with a paper entitled _Application of computational techniques in the visualization and analysis of stratigraphic data_, by Domenico Santoro and Julian Bogdani.

---
