As part of the Cerealia Festival, on Saturday 12 October at 5:30 PM, in Tarquinia, at the Palazzo dei Priori on Via dell'Archetto 4, a meeting entitled "Cultural Connections between Italy and Albania" will be held.

The speakers at the meeting will be: Mario Bova, former Italian Ambassador to Tirana (1999-2003), and Julian Bogdani, director of the Italian-Albanian Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit, Sapienza Università di Roma.

The meeting, jointly organised by Omnia Tuscia, the Società Tarquiniense d'Arte e Storia, and the Embassy of Albania in Italy, will be attended by Anila Bitri Lani, Ambassador of the Republic of Albania in Italy, who will deliver the institutional welcome address.

The event is part of the activities of the 14th edition of the Cerealia Festival.

### Programme

**Institutional welcome addresses**

- **Alessandra Sileoni**. President, Società Tarquiniense d'Arte e Storia
- **Anila Bitri Lani**. Ambassador of Albania in Italy
- **Enrico Cesarini**. President, "Omnia Tuscia"

**Introduction:**
- **Paola Sarcina**. Director, Cerealia Festival

**Speakers**
- **Mario Bova**. Former Ambassador of Italy to Tirana (1999-2003)  
  _Albanian culture in relations with Italy_
- **Julian Bogdani**. Sapienza Università di Roma. Director of the Italian-Albanian Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit  
  _Italian culture in relations with Albania_

**Moderator**
- **Costantino Moretti**. Secretary General, "Omnia Tuscia"
