As part of the PRIN 2022 project "[FortNet: Fortification and population network in coastal Chaonia, Northern Epirus (Albania) between Iron Age and the Medieval period](https://fortnet.lad-sapienza.it/)", the international workshop entitled **"Far from the city: fortifications and organisational models on the margins of traditional historiography"** will be held at the [Royal Netherlands Institute in Rome (KNIR)](https://knir2025.wordpress.com/).

The workshop is co-organised by the KNIR (Tesse **Stek**), Sapienza Università di Roma (Julian **Bogdani**), and the University of Bologna (Enrico **Giorgi**), and will take place on **20 February 2026** from 10:30 AM to 5:00 PM at the [KNIR premises, Via Omero 10-12, Rome](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QAhtr2GG2bLfVUDBA).
