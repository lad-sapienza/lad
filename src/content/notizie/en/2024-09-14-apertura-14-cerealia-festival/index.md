On 14 September, the director of the Italian-Albanian Archaeological Mission (Sapienza - Albanian Institute of Archaeology) Julian Bogdani will take part in the opening ceremony of the 14th edition of the Cerealia Festival, organised by the cultural association **Music Theatre International - M.Th.I. ETS**, in collaboration with the **International Association of Mediterranean and Oriental Studies (ISMEO)** and the **Accademia Vivarium Novum**, under the patronage of the **Italian Ministry of Agriculture, Food Sovereignty and Forestry (MASAF)**, the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation (MAECI)**, the **Embassy of the Republic of Albania**, the **Lazio Region**, **Roma Capitale**, and the **Regional Association of Journalists for Agriculture, Food, Environment, Territory, Rural Tourism, Forests, Fisheries and Renewable Energy (ARGA) Lazio**.

The festival has received:
- the EFFE LABEL, European Festival of Excellence (from 2015 to 2025),
- the Medal of the President of the Republic (editions from 2017 to 2021, 2023).

The ceremony will be held at Villa Falconieri (Viale Borromini 5 | 00044 Frascati RM).

### Programme

**Welcome address**  
- Luigi Miraglia (president, Accademia Vivarium Novum)
- Paola Sarcina (director, Cerealia Festival; president, M.Th.I. ETS).

**Handover between Slovenia and Albania, in the presence of high diplomatic representatives in Italy**
- H.E. Matjaž Longar, Ambassador of the Republic of Slovenia,
- H.E. Anila Bitri Lani, Ambassador of the Republic of Albania.


**A look at historical and contemporary Albania**: round table with
- Adriano Rossi (president, ISMEO),
- Julian Bogdani (Sapienza University - Rome),
- Luigi Miraglia (president, Accademia Vivarium Novum),
- Gjergji Kajana (freelance Albanian journalist specialising in international relations).

Moderated by Paola Sarcina (president, MThI / director, Cerealia Festival)

**Gjitonia - songs of Arbëria**, concert by
- Rocco Marco Moccia, voice and guitar, 
- Francesco Magarò, frame drums, 
- Oreste Forestieri, flutes and mandolin
