On **8 and 9 November 2023**, the directors of the [Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit](../../ricerca/missione-archeologica-sapienza-a-cuka-e-ajtoit-albania/), Albana **Meta** and Julian **Bogdani**, will take part in the international conference "Arkeologjia shqiptare në 75-vjetorin e krijimit të saj: arritje, risi dhe perspektiva" (Albanian archaeology on its 75th anniversary: achievements, innovations and perspectives).

The event is organised by the Academy of Sciences of Albania and the Academy of Sciences of Kosovo, in collaboration with the Institute of Archaeology of Kosovo, the Ministry of Culture of the Republic of Albania, and the Albanian Institute of Archaeology. The event can be followed remotely via Zoom, at: [https://us02web.zoom.us/j/89751945823?pwd=VXllbGpnUUVUOThHUU1RekthVGRVZz09](https://us02web.zoom.us/j/89751945823?pwd=VXllbGpnUUVUOThHUU1RekthVGRVZz09)
