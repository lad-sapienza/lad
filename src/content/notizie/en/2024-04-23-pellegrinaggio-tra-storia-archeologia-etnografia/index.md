On **23 April 2024**, LAD member Erasmo di Fonso will take part in the workshop "Pilgrimage between History, Archaeology and Ethnography – The Sanctuaries of the Santissima Trinità di Vallepietra (Rome) and San Michele al Monte Tancia (Rieti)", organised by Sapienza Università di Roma, with a paper entitled _Collection, cataloguing and information management: databases of the sacred within the PNRR project "The Sacred Landscape along the Aniene and the Via Tiburtina Valeria (from Alta Sabina to the Apennines): archaeological contexts and ancient texts"_.

The paper will focus on the work carried out on the rationalisation and structuring of a geodatabase designed to bring together the project's data, and on its future use for the development of a website aimed at promoting the area.

The workshop is organised as part of the PNRR WP5 - Spoke 1 project.

The full session programme is available at the following link: [https://web.uniroma1.it/lettere/sites/default/files/download/Workshop%2019%20aprile%202024.pdf](https://web.uniroma1.it/lettere/sites/default/files/download/Workshop%2019%20aprile%202024.pdf)
