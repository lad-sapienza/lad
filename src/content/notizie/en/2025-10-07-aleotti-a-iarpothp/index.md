As part of the [Sapienza Archaeological Mission in Albania](../../ricerca/missione-archeologica-sapienza-a-cuka-e-ajtoit-albania/) and of the PRIN 2022 project [FortNet. Fortification and population network in coastal Chaonia, Northern Epirus (Albania) between Iron Age and the Medieval period: a longue durée approach to the study of settlements, economic and defensive systems](https://lad.saras.uniroma1.it/ricerca/fortnet-project-prin-2022/), **Nadia Aleotti** will take part, from 6 to 9 October 2025, in the seventh edition of the [IARPotHP International Conference - International Association for Research on Pottery of the Hellenistic Period](https://iarpothp.org/conferences/), to be held at the Université libre de Bruxelles-ULB and the Royal Museums of Art and History in Brussels, with a paper entitled "Pottery from the Hellenistic votive deposit from the "Palace" of Çuka e Ajtoit (Southern Albania). A case of ceramic productions intended for sacred contexts in northern Epirus?"
