As part of the Sapienza Archaeological Mission in Albania and of the project "PRIN 2022 FortNet. Fortification and population network in coastal Chaonia, Northern Epirus (Albania) between Iron Age and the Medieval period: a longue durée approach to the study of settlements, economic and defensive systems", on Thursday 27 November Nadia Aleotti will take part in the workshop "Transport containers and trade in the Adriatic area from Antiquity to the Middle Ages" (Campus Universitario Chieti Scalo, 27–28 November 2025).

Nadia Aleotti's paper, scheduled for **Thursday 27 November at 4:20 PM**, is entitled "Corinthian amphorae from Çuka e Ajtoit and Butrint: models, typologies and production from the Archaic to the Hellenistic period. Preliminary results and research prospects".

The workshop is organised by the University "G. d'Annunzio" Chieti-Pescara – Department of Sciences, the International Centre for Studies on the History and Archaeology of the Adriatic, and the Centre for Adriatic Archaeology Studies.
