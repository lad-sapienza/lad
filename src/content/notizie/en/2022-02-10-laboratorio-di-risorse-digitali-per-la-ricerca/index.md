As part of the PhD programme in History, Anthropology and Religions at Sapienza Università di Roma, starting on 7 March 2022 the RDR Lab (Laboratorio di Risorse Digitali per la Ricerca — Digital Research Resources Lab) will be launched. The 2022 edition of the lab, titled "Experiences. Projects. Tools", will be curated by [Julian Bogdani](https://purl.org/lad/jb) and [Federica Favino](https://corsidilaurea.uniroma1.it/it/users/julianbogdaniuniroma1it), both researchers at Sapienza Università di Roma and members of the [SARAS Department](https://saras.uniroma1.it/).

Through a series of bimonthly meetings, topics related to the use of digital resources applied to research in the humanities will be addressed.

The meetings will be held **in person** in Aula 2 of the SARAS Department (Faculty of Letters and Philosophy - CU003, 1st floor, Art section) and **remotely** via [Zoom](https://uniroma1.zoom.us/j/83808083152?pwd=Z0FWbGM5TmJqRGZXaVZBWUJ6d0xVUT09).

### RDR Lab 2022 calendar

**NB: The calendar may be subject to changes in the coming weeks. Keep an eye on this post!**

- **7 March, 1-3 PM**: Renata Ago (Sapienza Università di Roma) and Domizia D'Erasmo (Sapienza Università di Roma), _Moving around Rome in the eighteenth century_ (research diary for the volume: R. Ago, _Il diritto alla città. Roma nel Settecento_, Viella, Rome 2021)
- **21 March, 1-3 PM**: Julian Bogdani and Domizia D'Erasmo, _The GIS system of Napoleonic cartography of Egypt: new data for historical and archaeological research_
- **4 April, 1-3 PM**: Julian Bogdani (Sapienza Università di Roma), _GIS: Geographic Information Systems_
- **11 April, 1-3 PM**: Stefano dall'Aglio, Università Ca' Foscari, Venice, _The Ottavio Falconieri Project_
- **29 April, 3:30-5:30 PM**: Giovanna Ceserani (Stanford University), _The Grand Tour project at Stanford_
- **2 May, 1-3 PM**: Alessandra Celati (University of Verona), _Heresy runs through the network_ (research diary for the volume: A. Celati, _The World of Girolamo Donzellini. A Network of Heterodox Physicians in Sixteenth-Century Venice_, Routledge, New York, 2022)
- **16 May, 1-3 PM**: Enzo Cocca (CNR-ISPC): _'R'. A software environment for statistical computing_
