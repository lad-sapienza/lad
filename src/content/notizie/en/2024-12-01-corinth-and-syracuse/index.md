On the upcoming 5-7 December, the International Conference "Corinth and Syracuse: Connections, Exchanges, Influences" will be held at the Museo Archeologico Regionale "Paolo Orsi" in Syracuse. Nadia Aleotti will take part with a paper entitled "A colonial Artemis? Aspects of the cult of the goddess in Syracuse and Corcyra" (with B. Risposi, University of Milan).

The link to follow the conference remotely can be obtained by contacting [corinth.syracuse2024@gmail.com](mailto:corinth.syracuse2024@gmail.com).
