From **9 to 12 October 2023**, the director of the [Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit](../../ricerca/missione-archeologica-sapienza-a-cuka-e-ajtoit-albania/), Julian **Bogdani**, and Dr Nadia **Aleotti** (Research Fellow in Archaeological Research Methodologies at Sapienza), will take part in the [sixth edition of the international conference of "The International Association for Research on Pottery of the Hellenistic Period"](https://iarpothp.org/conferences_en_6.html), to be held in Catania and Syracuse, with a paper entitled "Hellenistic pottery from Çuka e Ajtoit (Konispol, Albania): old and new excavations, old and new methods".
