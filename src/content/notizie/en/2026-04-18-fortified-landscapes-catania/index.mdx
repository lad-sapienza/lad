On Wednesday 29 and Thursday 30 April 2026, the co-director of the Italian-Albanian Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit, Julian Bogdani, will take part with a paper in the international conference "Fortified Landscapes: Towards a Research Project" at the University of Catania.

- 18:00 | Institutional Greetings
- 18:30 | Welcome and speakers introduction, opening remarks on the workshop's general themes
- 20:30 | Social dinner

_Mapping memory and myth: Surface Survey in the Upper Acheron Valley_

_Excavating the Fortified Site of Sistrouni: Preliminary Results from the Upper Acheron Valley_
