To conclude the [teaching lab on the use of the Laser Aided Profiler (LAP)](../2025-01-17-laboratorio-didattico-lap/), in collaboration with the [Sapienza Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit (Albania)](../../ricerca/missione-archeologica-sapienza-a-cuka-e-ajtoit-albania/) and as part of the project [PRIN 2022 "FortNet. Fortification and population network in coastal Chaonia, Northern Epirus (Albania) between Iron Age and the Medieval period: a longue durée approach to the study of settlements, economic and defensive systems"](../../ricerca/fortnet-project-prin-2022/), LAD is organising a round table on the theme: "Laser Aided Profiler: Innovations and potential of the tool. Comparing experiences".

The round table will be held on Friday **14 March 2025** from 9:45 AM to 1:00 PM, in the **Ernesto Buonaiuti** hall, on the third floor of the Faculty of Letters and Philosophy, Sapienza Università di Roma.

It will also be possible to attend the event online, via the link: [https://meet.google.com/dag-xxdy-awv](https://meet.google.com/dag-xxdy-awv).


## Programme

**Sharing experiences** (9:45 - 11:00 AM)  
Moderators: Nadia Aleotti, Francesca Di Maria and Nicola Lapacciana  
Presenters: 
- Nadia **Aleotti**, Sapienza Università di Roma
- Mario **Indelicato**, University of Catania
- Fabiana **Cerasa**, University of Catania
- Nicola **Lapacciana**, Sapienza Università di Roma
- Francesca **Di Maria**, Sapienza Università di Roma
- Lorenzo **Cardarelli**, Sapienza Università di Roma

**Open discussion** (11:00 AM - 1:00 PM)  
Moderator: Julian **Bogdani**  
The discussion will address, among others, the following topics:
- Reliability and precision of data acquisition
- Standardisation and best practices for the use of the LAP
- Theoretical implications and impact on "trowel's edge" interpretation
- The risks of an overly early approach
- Automatic classification algorithms and their impact on the pottery-study process
- Limits and future prospects

The following will also join the discussion:
- Stefano **Bertoldi**, University of Siena 
- Antonio **Ferrandes**, Sapienza Università di Roma
- Alessandro **Vanzetti**, Sapienza Università di Roma
- Enrico **Zanini**, University of Siena

Students and colleagues, both in person and connected online, are invited to take an active part in the discussion.
