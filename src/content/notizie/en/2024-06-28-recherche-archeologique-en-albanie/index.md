On **28 June 2024**, the director of the Italian-Albanian Archaeological Mission (Sapienza - Albanian Institute of Archaeology), Julian Bogdani, will take part in the conference "Actualité de la recherche archéologique en Albanie", organised at the Collège de France (3 rue d'Ulm, 75005, Paris) by E. Nallbani, F. Quantin and S. Shpuza, with a paper entitled _New researches at Çuka e Ajtoit/Kestria: a fortified site facing Kerkyra_.
