GARR, the consortium that manages the cloud infrastructure hosting the Laboratory's servers, has reported **today, 2 October 2026**, that some of its cloud services, together with its identity and dashboard services, are currently unreachable due to a connectivity problem with Southern Italy. GARR's NOC (Network Operations Center) engineers are working to restore connectivity as soon as possible.

As a result, **LAD IT services hosted on this infrastructure are currently unreachable**. No restoration time is known at the moment.

**What is temporarily unavailable**

- Platforms and online databases hosted on our cloud servers (including excavation databases and related tools)
- File storage services (Object Store)

This is a network reachability problem, not an intervention on the content: no data loss is expected. We invite you to plan your activities accordingly, avoiding critical operations on our online systems until service is restored.

We apologise for the inconvenience. **We will publish updates on this page as soon as we receive new information from GARR.**
