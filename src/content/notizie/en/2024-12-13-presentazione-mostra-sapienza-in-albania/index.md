On Friday **13 December 2024**, at **10:00 AM**, at the **Aula Simonetti of the SARAS Department**, on the third floor of the [Faculty of Letters and Philosophy (CU003)](https://virtualtour.uniroma1.it/?building=CU003&c=1), the exhibition "[Sapienza in Albania. One hundred years since Luigi Maria Ugolini's first visit to the country](2024-12-09-mostra-per-pannelli-sapienza-in-albania)" will be presented, set up at the premises of the [Museo dell'Arte Classica of Sapienza](https://web.uniroma1.it/polomuseale/museo-arte-classica) (9 December 2024 - 31 January 2024).

**Speakers**

- **Antonella Polimeni**  
    Rector of Sapienza Università di Roma
- **Alessandro Saggioro**  
    Director of the Department of History, Anthropology, Religions,
    Arts and Performing Arts of Sapienza Università di Roma
- **Anila Bitri Lani**  
    Ambassador of the Republic of Albania in Italy
- **Gianluca Biscardi**  
    Head of Section II: Archaeology, DGDP VI  
    Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Italian Republic
- **Roberto Raieli**  
    Director of the Library of the Department of History, Anthropology,
    Religions, Arts and Performing Arts of Sapienza Università di Roma
- **Julian Bogdani**  
    Director of the Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit (Albania)
    Sapienza Università di Roma

A guided visit to the exhibition will follow the meeting.

**Colleagues and interested students are invited to attend.**

> The exhibition was organised by the [Italian-Albanian Archaeological Mission (Sapienza Università di Roma and the Institute of Archaeology of Tirana) at Çuka e Ajtoit](https://purl.org/lad/caj), with the support of the Wikimedia Foundation and in collaboration with the Embassy of the Republic of Albania in Italy.

For information, please write to [julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it](mailto:julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it).
