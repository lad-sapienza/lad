On Thursday 25 March 2026, the co-director of the Italian-Albanian Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit, Julian Bogdani, will take part with a paper in the conference "Viti Arkeologjik 2025" (Archaeological Year 2025) at the Academy of Sciences of the Republic of Albania. This conference will present the results of the 2025 excavation and research campaign at the site of Çuka e Ajtoit.

---
