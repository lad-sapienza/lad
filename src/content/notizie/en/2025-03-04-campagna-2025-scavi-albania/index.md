The 2025 campaign of excavations and archaeological research of the Albanian Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit in Albania will last approximately **four weeks**, in a period **indicatively** between **9 June and 6 July 2025**.

Fieldwork activities will include **archaeological survey**, **excavation**, and **documentation**, both **topographic** and of the **materials** recovered.

The excavation will focus in particular on a semi-private domestic context of the **Hellenistic period** (3rd-2nd centuries BC) and a Byzantine-period church (12th-13th centuries AD), the so-called Basilica, while the surveys will cover the entire surface of the archaeological site and sites of archaeological interest in the surrounding area.

For more details on the mission's scientific activities, please refer to the [page dedicated to the activities carried out so far](https://purl.org/lad/caj).

### How to take part

You can express your interest in any case by filling out the form available at the following address: [https://forms.gle/jFn8eYrDEm4G9r1f8](https://forms.gle/jFn8eYrDEm4G9r1f8)

### Practical information

The [site of Çuka e Ajtoit](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ViLNNFq9N9GethfL7) is located in southern Albania and is easily reachable from Italy by plane (to Tirana, with Wizz Air, Ryanair, Ita Airways, etc. connections) and bus (Tirana – [Konispol](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HQRXo2M3vQWiXGuC6)), or by plane to Corfu (Greece, with Ryanair connections) and then by boat to Saranda.

To travel to Albania, Italian, European and many other citizens do **not** need a passport — a valid identity card for travel abroad is sufficient.

The Mission covers all food and lodging expenses on site.
