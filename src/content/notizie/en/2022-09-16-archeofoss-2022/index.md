On **Thursday 22** and **Friday 23 September 2022**, the **sixteenth edition of the International ArcheoFOSS Conference: Open software, hardware, processes, data and formats in the archaeological research** will take place.

This year's edition is hosted by Sapienza's [DigiLab Research Centre](https://digilab.uniroma1.it/), in the Giorgio Levi della Vida hall, at the "ex Vetrerie Sciarra" site of the Faculty of Letters and Philosophy, realised with the support of the [Directorate-General for Education, Research and Cultural Institutes, Ministry of Culture](https://dger.beniculturali.it/) and with the support of [LAD](https://lad.saras.uniroma1.it).

The conference is structured into **eight _panels_** addressing specific topics relating to the application of the _open science_ model to archaeological and, more broadly, humanities research, and to the development, application and dissemination of free and _open source_ digital technologies for scientific research in the humanities, with the participation of a wide and international audience of speakers.

The conference proceedings can also be followed _online_.

For information, please contact Julian Bogdani: [julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it](mailto:julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it)
