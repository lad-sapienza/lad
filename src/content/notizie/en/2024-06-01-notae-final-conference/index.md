On **31 May 2024**, LAD director Julian Bogdani will take part in the final conference of the ERC NOTAE project, "Segni, testi, _data_, percorsi di ricerca attraverso la cultura scritta documentaria tardoantica e alto medievale / Signs, Texts, Data Research Routes through the Documentary Written Culture in Late Antiquity and Early Middle Ages", with a paper entitled _The activities of LAD, Laboratorio di Archeologia Digitale at Sapienza, between research and teaching: an assessment and future prospects_.
