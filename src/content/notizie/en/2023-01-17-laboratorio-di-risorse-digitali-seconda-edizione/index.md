As part of the PhD programme in History, Anthropology and Religions at Sapienza Università di Roma, starting on 18 January 2023 the second edition of the RDR Lab (Laboratorio di Risorse Digitali per la Ricerca — Digital Research Resources Lab) will be held. The 2023 edition of the lab, titled "Experiences. Projects. Tools", will be curated by [Julian Bogdani](https://purl.org/lad/jb) and [Federica Favino](https://corsidilaurea.uniroma1.it/it/users/federicafavinouniroma1it), both researchers at Sapienza Università di Roma and members of the [SARAS Department](https://saras.uniroma1.it/).

Through a series of meetings, topics related to the use of digital resources applied to research in the humanities will be addressed.

The meetings will be held **in person** at Sapienza Università di Roma, DigiLab, in the former Vetrerie Sciarra building, conference room (first floor), 1:00-3:00 PM, and **remotely** via [Google Meet](https://meet.google.com/wmp-xffy-njv).

[**Download the lab poster in PDF format**](/notizie/it/2023-01-17-laboratorio-di-risorse-digitali-seconda-edizione/RDR_Lab2_2023.pdf)

### RDR Lab 2023 calendar

- 18 January: Paolo **Monella** - Andrea **Consalvi**, _The Latin Grammarians Collection: methodological aspects of a digital resource_.
- 8 February: Emmanuel **Betta**, _Digitising broadcasting sources: the experience of the Radio Onda Rossa archive_.
- 22 February: Chiara **Piva**, _Virtual reality, augmented reality and mixed reality: comparing experiences from the perspective of humanities studies_.
- 8 March: Umberto **Longo**, _Digital technologies and the history of territory_.
- 22 March: Antonella **Ghignoli**, _Graphic signs between words and symbols: late antique and early medieval documentation in the NOTAE project_.
- 5 April: Federica **Perazzini**, _Computational criticism: from distant reading to false movement_.
- ~~19~~ 26 April: Damiano **Garofalo** - Valerio **Coladonato**, _Digital Humanities for the study of cinema: two research and teaching experiences_.
- 3 May: Elisabetta **Corsi** - Silvia **Piccini** (CNR, Pisa) - Simone **Marchi** (CNR, Pisa), _The 'Unus non sufficit orbis' digital library: development of the portal and lexicographic applications_. 
- 17 May: Maria Grazia **Berlangieri**, _Ontology of digital data: from sources to the interpolation of artificial intelligence_.

**Unless otherwise specified, all speakers are affiliated with Sapienza Università di Roma. For information: [julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it](mailto:julian.bogdani@uniroma1.it) - [federica.favino@uniroma1.it](mailto:federica.favino@uniroma1.it)**
