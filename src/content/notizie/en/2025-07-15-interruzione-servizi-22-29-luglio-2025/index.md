Due to major extraordinary maintenance work on the electrical system of the INFN-LNS data centre in Catania, which hosts all of LAD's online services, a complete shutdown of the infrastructure has been scheduled for the period between 22 and 29 July 2025. We apologise for the inconvenience and hope this does not cause too many problems.

### ⚠️ Notice of LAD Service Interruption – 22-29 July 2025 ⚠️

- 📅 **Scheduled downtime period**: From 22 to 29 July 2025
- 📍 **Location**: INFN-LNS Datacenter, Catania (hosts all services offered by LAD)
- 🛠️ **Reason for the interruption**: Major extraordinary electrical maintenance work will require the complete shutdown of the infrastructure.
- 🚫 **Consequences**: During this period, all LAD services will be temporarily unavailable, including websites, databases, APIs and related digital tools.

🙏 We apologise for the inconvenience and thank you for your understanding.
We hope this interruption does not cause serious difficulties for your activities.
