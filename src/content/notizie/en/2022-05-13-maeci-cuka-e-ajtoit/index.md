The [Italian-Albanian archaeological mission at Çuka e Ajtoit, Konispol, Albania](../../ricerca/missione-archeologica-sapienza-a-cuka-e-ajtoit-albania/) of Sapienza Università di Roma will operate in 2022 with the **official recognition of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation**.

The granting of the **Institutional Recognition** was resolved by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, following consultation with the Advisory Working Group for the Promotion of Italian Culture and Language Abroad, Archaeological Missions section.

The 2022 campaign, preparations for which have entered their final stage, will take place between late June and late July 2022, for a duration of about a month, and will involve researchers and students from Sapienza, as well as researchers from the Albanian Institute of Archaeology and students from the University of Tirana.

More information will be made available soon.
