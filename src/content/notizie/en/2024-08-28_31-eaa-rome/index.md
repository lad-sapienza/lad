At the upcoming international conference of the European Association of Archaeologists (EAA), to be held in Rome at Sapienza Università di Roma on 28-31 August, several members of the Sapienza Archaeological Mission at Çuka e Ajtoit will present papers:

- **Francesca D'Ambola**, 'Trojan Wanderers Between East And West: Approaching Sacred Landscapes In Northern Epirus'  
  - Session 'Sacred Landscapes in Context: Creation, Development, and Conceptualisation'  
  - Thursday 29 August 2024, 2:15 PM  
  - Aula Vetri of the Faculty of Letters and Philosophy (CU003), outdoor area.
- **Julian Bogdani**, *Fortified Landscapes in Ancient Epirus: New Research in The Hellenistic Site of Çuka e Ajtoit (Albania)*.  
  - Session: 'Bridging the Walls: Comparative Perspectives On Fortified Settlements'.  
  - Saturday 31 August 2024, 3:15 PM  
  - Aula I of the Faculty of Letters and Philosophy (CU003), 1st floor.
- **Nadia Aleotti**, *Northern Epirus Between the Classical and the Hellenistic Period: The Case of Çuka e Ajtoit, Butrint and Their Regional Context*  
  - Session: 'Adriatic Bridge - Unravelling Political, Social, and Cultural Transformations in the Adriatic-Ionian, and Balkan Region in the Last Millenium BC'  
  - Saturday 31 August 2024, 10:00 AM  
  - Aula III of the Faculty of Letters and Philosophy (CU003), 1st floor.

We would also like to point out other papers of interest for the archaeology of Epirus:

- **Veronica Castignani**. *Fortified Landscapes Of Hellenistic Epirus: Fortifications, Mobility And Control Of Space*  
  - Session: 'Cooperating or Competing? The Organisation of Space and Power Using Networks and Systems of Fortifications'
  - Thursday 29 August 2024, 2:45 PM
  - Aula Lucchesi of the Department of Earth Sciences (CU005), 1st floor
- **Federica Carbotti**, *Between Mountains and Sea. Preliminary Data From the Survey of Three Protohistoric Fortified Settlements in Southern Albania*.  
  - Session: 'Bridging the Walls: Comparative Perspectives On Fortified Settlements'.  
  - Saturday 31 August 2024, 2:00 PM  
  - Aula I of the Faculty of Letters and Philosophy (CU003), 1st floor.
