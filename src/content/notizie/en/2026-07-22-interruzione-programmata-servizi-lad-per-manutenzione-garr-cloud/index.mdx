# Scheduled interruption of LAD services for GARR Cloud maintenance

*28–30 July 2026*

GARR (the consortium that manages the cloud infrastructure hosting the Laboratory's servers) has announced a scheduled shutdown of the Catania data centre (region `garr-ct1`), necessary for extraordinary maintenance work on the UPS system.

As a result, **LAD services hosted on this infrastructure will be temporarily unreachable** starting at **4:00 PM on Tuesday 28 July** until **Thursday 30 July 2026**.

**What will be temporarily unavailable**

- Platforms and online databases hosted on our cloud servers (including excavation databases and related tools)
- File storage services (Object Store)

We invite you to plan your activities accordingly, avoiding scheduling critical operations on our online systems during this time window. No data will be lost: this is a controlled shutdown of the physical infrastructure, not an intervention on the content.

We apologise for the inconvenience. For any urgent needs during the interruption period, you can contact us at the Laboratory's usual contact details.
